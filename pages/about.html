OUR JOURNEY

A Decade of Excellence

2018

Global Beginnings

Began operations as an international courier company, laying the foundation for our global logistics network.

2021

Expansion & Mobility

FRelocated to a new headquarters in Kollupitiya and officially launched EGX Fleet, marking our entry into dedicated transportation services.

2022

Storage & Scale

Inaugurated EGX Space, establishing our first major warehousing facility in Maguruwela.

2023

Heavy Lifting & Reach

Extended the EGX Fleet with heavy-duty prime movers and expanded our regional footprint by opening the EGX Biyagama Branch.

2024

The Logistics Hub

Opened the EGX Logistic Center and established new subsidiaries HD SwiftMove Pvt Ltd and Nexlog Freight Flow Pvt Ltd.
